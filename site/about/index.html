About

Komo E.G.C Inc.

A Windsor-based licensed electrical contractor, incorporated in 2019, working for general contractors, businesses and homeowners across Essex County.

What we do

Komo E.G.C Inc. installs and upgrades electrical systems for commercial, industrial and residential buildings: tenant fit-outs, restaurant kitchens, 347/600V three-phase power, machine hookups, warehouse LED retrofits, panel upgrades and generator transfer switches.

Most of our work comes through general contractors and business owners who need the electrical scope done on schedule, filed with ESA and closed out with the paperwork in order.

How we work

  • Every job filed with the Electrical Safety Authority under our own licence
  • Line-item quotes with exclusions written down
  • Crews scheduled to your milestones
  • Certificate of Acceptance handed over at closeout
