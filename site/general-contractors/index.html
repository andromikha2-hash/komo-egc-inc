Prequalification

How to vet an electrical sub in Ontario.

Five checks before you award an electrical scope. We meet all five.

  1. ECRA/ESA contractor licenceOnly a Licensed Electrical Contractor can be hired for electrical work in Ontario. Ask for the 7-digit number and check it on ESA's lookup. Ours is #7015083.
  2. Who files the ESA notificationThe contractor doing the work must file it. Make sure it's filed under the sub's licence, not left to you.
  3. WSIB clearance and insuranceGet a current WSIB clearance certificate and a certificate of insurance before the sub mobilizes.
  4. Commercial and three-phase experienceFit-outs, kitchens and 600V distribution are different from house wiring. Ask what they've done in the last year.
  5. Itemized pricingA line-item quote with exclusions stated is the only way to compare bids and price change orders cleanly.
