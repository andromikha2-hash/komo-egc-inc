Licensing, scope and process

Straight answers.

Licence #7015083 can be checked on ESA's contractor lookup.

Is Komo E.G.C Inc. a licensed electrical contractor in Ontario?

Yes. Komo E.G.C Inc. holds ECRA/ESA Electrical Contractor Licence #7015083. You can verify it on the Electrical Safety Authority's contractor lookup.

Do you sub-contract to general contractors?

Yes. We take electrical scopes from general contractors and project managers on commercial fit-outs, restaurants, retail, office and warehouse projects across Windsor-Essex. Send the drawings and schedule for a line-item price.

Do you work on 600V three-phase systems?

Yes. We install and upgrade 347/600V three-phase services and distribution, including step-down transformers to 120/208V, machine feeds, motor controls and VFDs.

Which areas do you serve?

Windsor, Tecumseh, LaSalle, Lakeshore, Amherstburg and Essex, plus the rest of Essex County, Ontario.

Who files the ESA notification and books inspections?

We do. Komo E.G.C Inc. files the ESA notification of work under licence #7015083, books inspections and hands over the Certificate of Acceptance at closeout.

Can you provide WSIB clearance and a certificate of insurance?

Yes. We provide a WSIB clearance certificate and a certificate of insurance for your project file before mobilization.

How do you price a job?

From drawings or a site walk. You get a line-item quote with exclusions stated, so you can compare, carry and change-order cleanly.

Do you do residential work?

Yes, mainly service and panel upgrades, generator transfer switches and renovation wiring. Most of our work is commercial and industrial.
