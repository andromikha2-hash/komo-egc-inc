Questions

Generators & Transfer Switches: common questions.

Can I plug a portable generator into my house?

Only through a transfer switch, or an approved interlock with a generator inlet. Plugging into an outlet can backfeed the utility lines and endanger line workers. We install the inlet and transfer equipment and file the work with ESA.

Do you install the generator itself?

We handle the electrical side: transfer switch, wiring and connection. For gas-fired standby units, a licensed gas fitter makes the gas connection.
