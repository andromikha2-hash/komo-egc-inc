Commercial tenant fit-out electrical, shell to Certificate of Acceptance.

Komo E.G.C Inc. takes tenant fit-outs from bare shell to an energized, inspected space. We work from your electrical drawings or the landlord's base-building information, price the scope line by line, and sequence rough-in so insulation, drywall and ceilings are never waiting on us.

What's included

  • New panels, sub-panels and distribution for the unit
  • Branch circuits, receptacles and dedicated equipment circuits

  • Emergency lighting and exit signs

  • Storefront lighting and exterior sign power

Questions

Commercial Tenant Fit-Outs: common questions.

Can you work to the landlord's tenant criteria?

Yes. We review the landlord's tenant criteria and base-building electrical before quoting, so the fit-out matches what the building can supply.

Who files the ESA notification on a fit-out?

We do, under Komo E.G.C Inc.'s ECRA/ESA contractor licence #7015083. We book the inspections and hand over the Certificate of Acceptance at closeout.

Can you price from drawings before a site visit?

Yes. Send the electrical drawings and we return a line-item price with exclusions listed. A site walk confirms existing conditions before the contract is signed.
