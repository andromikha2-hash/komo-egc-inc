Questions

Restaurant & Kitchen Electrical: common questions.

Do you wire hood control panels?

Yes. We wire hood fans, make-up air and the hood control panel, and coordinate with the hood installer so interlocks are connected before inspection.

Can you tell me if my service can handle new kitchen equipment?

Yes. We review the load from the equipment schedule and tell you whether the service, the panel or both need an upgrade before you order equipment.

Do you work from the kitchen supplier's drawings?

Yes. We work from the supplier's equipment schedule and rough-in drawings so connection points land where the equipment sits.
