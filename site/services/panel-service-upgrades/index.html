Questions

Panel & Service Upgrades: common questions.

Do I need a permit for a panel upgrade?

Yes. In Ontario, panel and service work needs an ESA notification. Komo E.G.C Inc. files it under licence #7015083 and books the inspection.

Do you coordinate with the utility?

Yes. Service upgrades need the utility to disconnect and reconnect. We arrange it with the utility serving the address.

What is thermal imaging for?

Infrared scanning finds loose connections and overloaded breakers by the heat they give off, before they fail. It is done with the equipment energized and under load.
