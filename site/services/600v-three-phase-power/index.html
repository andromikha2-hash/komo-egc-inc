347/600V three-phase services, distribution and transformers.

Industrial and larger commercial buildings in Windsor-Essex commonly run on 347/600V three-phase power. Komo E.G.C Inc. installs and upgrades that power: service entrance equipment, distribution, step-down transformers to 120/208V, and the feeders that tie it all together.

Questions

600V Three-Phase Power: common questions.

Do you work on 600V systems?

Yes. Komo E.G.C Inc. installs and upgrades 347/600V three-phase services and distribution, including step-down transformers to 120/208V, machine feeds, motor controls and VFDs.

Can you add a transformer to feed 120/208V equipment in a 600V building?

Yes. We size, install and connect the step-down transformer and the panel it feeds, filed with ESA under our licence.

Do you coordinate the utility shutdown?

Yes. We coordinate the disconnect and reconnect with the utility serving the address and schedule the shutdown around your operations.
