For general contractors & project managers

Your schedule is the contract.

An electrical sub that misses rough-in holds up insulation, drywall and every trade behind it. We plan crews against your milestones, show up when the board says, and close out with the paperwork in order.

Schedule

Staffed to your schedule

Crew size and start dates are set from your project schedule before mobilization. Rough-in is sequenced ahead of insulation and board so no trade waits on us.

Code

Built to the OESC

Installed to the Ontario Electrical Safety Code, with conduit, fill and bonding laid out to pass the first inspection.

Inspections

ESA handled by us

We file the ESA notification under our licence, book inspections against your timeline, and meet the inspector on site.

Estimating

Line-item pricing

Quotes are itemized by scope so you can compare, carry and change-order cleanly. Exclusions are written down, not discovered later.

Every sub-contract includes

  • ESA notification of work filed under licence #7015083
  • WSIB clearance certificate before mobilization
  • Certificate of insurance for your project file
  • Long-lead material flagged at award, not at rough-in
  • Marked-up drawings for as-builts at closeout
  • ESA Certificate of Acceptance in the closeout package

How a scope runs

  1. Drawing review and site walkE-sheets, panel schedules and site conditions checked against the scope.
  2. Line-item quoteItemized price, exclusions stated, lead-time items called out.
  3. ESA notification and utility coordinationFiled under our licence; utility disconnects and reconnects arranged.
  4. Rough-inSequenced to your schedule and inspected before cover.
  5. Trim-out and energizeDevices, fixtures, equipment connections, panel directories.
  6. Final inspection and closeoutCertificate of Acceptance and as-builts handed over.

Questions GCs and owners ask

Straight answers.

The details a GC needs for prequalification and an owner needs before signing.

Is Komo E.G.C Inc. a licensed electrical contractor in Ontario?

Yes. Komo E.G.C Inc. holds ECRA/ESA Electrical Contractor Licence #7015083. You can verify it on the Electrical Safety Authority's contractor lookup.

Do you sub-contract to general contractors?

Yes. We take electrical scopes from general contractors and project managers on commercial fit-outs, restaurants, retail, office and warehouse projects across Windsor-Essex. Call with the scope and schedule for a line-item price.

Do you work on 600V three-phase systems?

Yes. We install and upgrade 347/600V three-phase services and distribution, including step-down transformers to 120/208V, machine feeds, motor controls and VFDs.

Are you available 24/7?

Yes. Komo E.G.C Inc. is open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Call 647-767-7815 any time, day or night.

Which areas do you serve?

Windsor, Tecumseh, LaSalle, Lakeshore, Amherstburg and Essex, plus the rest of Essex County, Ontario.
